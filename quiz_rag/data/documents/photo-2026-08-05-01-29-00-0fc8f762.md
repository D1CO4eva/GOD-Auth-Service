### Srimad Bhagavatam

- Unique: has **5 more lakshanAs**
- **Ashraya** is the main subject
- Each Canto covers a dominant Lakshana
- With 12 Cantos and 10 LakshanAs, **the first two Cantos are the Preface and Introduction.**
- Each canto will have many chapters which deal with a distinct subject or a story or a distinct portion of the story in line with the canto’s characteristic subject (lakshanā).

---

### The 6 Questions

Suta Pauranika was invited to speak on our Puranas during the relevant periods of the yagna. Shaunaka Rishis ask 6 questions to Suta Pauranika to elicit the essence of all scriptures.

1. What is the ultimate good for the people in this Kali Yuga?
2. Why did Bhagavan descend as the son of Devaki and Vasudeva?
3. Please describe the Gambheera leela of Bhagavan [His leela of Creation, sustenance and dissolution of the universe].
4. Describe all his incarnations.
5. Describe the leelas Bhagavan did along with Balarama
6. Where did Dharma take refuge after Bhagavan’s leela samvaraṇa (departure)?
