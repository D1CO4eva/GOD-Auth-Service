### “Others may have a holy book; ours is a huge library!”
– H.H. Maharanyam Sri Sri Muralidhara Swamiji

**‘Sanātana’** “always (sadā)” or “that which exists (bhava)”; eternal, ever-present
**‘Dharma’** “an essential quality” or “true innate nature” of an entity

---

### ‘Sanātana’ ‘Dharma’ is...

- Eternal, Universal, Cosmic Law - the natural order – one which can never be destroyed.
- An eternal “way of life” – codes of both internal and external conduct, ethics, and responsibilities.
- The path to realizing lasting happiness through aligning thoughts, speech, and action with Universal Values, in line with scriptures.

##### vedaishecha sarvai: ahameva vedya:
###### –Srimad Bhagavad Gita 15:15
“I (Bhagavan) am known through all the Vedas”

### Our Grand Shastras:
**Pramanas | Truth as it is...**

- **Vedas**
  - Means “knowledge”
  - Divine knowledge, Root of creation
  - Rig – Hymn in Praise
  - Yajur – Worship with Sacrifice
  - Sama – Peace to Minds (music)
  - Atharva – Mantras to ward off Evil

- **Itihāsās** (2)
  - Srimad Rāmāyanam – by Sri Valmiki Maharishi
  - Srimad Mahābhārātam – by Sri Veda Vyasa Maharishi

- **Purāṇās** (18)
  - Grouped by the Gunas (Sātvika, Rājasā, and Tāmasā)
  - Have a primary Deity/Focus

### The Great Vedas

Any good path (shown by the Vedas), and followed with faith and loyalty is supported for any devatā, to realize the true goal.

**4 Portions**
- 4-Upanishads
  Knowledge
  (Fruit)
- 3-Āranyaka
  Philosophy
  (Inner Fruit)
- 2-Brāhmana
  Rituals
  (Branch)
- 1-Samhitā
  Mantras
  (Trunk)
