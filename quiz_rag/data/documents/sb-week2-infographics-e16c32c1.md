## Sanatana Dharma

### Prasthāna Traya
The 3 pillars of Sanatana Dharma’s Philosophy

#### 1) Upanishads
- The primary authority on highest truth, and comes from the Vedas
- 108 mainstream Upanishads - 10 of them are considered the most important

#### 2) Srimad Bhagavad Gita
- The divine discussion between Bhagavan Sri Krishna and Arjuna on the Kurukshetra battlefield
- Part of Mahabharata (Itihasa), and has 18 chapters and 700 slokas

#### 3) Brahma Sutras
- A synthesis of all Upanishads by sage Veda Vyasa to show that they all carry the same message
- In the form of aphorisms (sutras are short, concise rules)

---

## Srimad Bhagavatam is the essence of all three pillars!

### How do we know this?

1) **Upanishads (Vedas):** Srimad Bhagavatam has a detailed commentary of the Gāyatri Mantra and contains the crux of all Upanishads.

2) **Srimad Bhagavad Gita (Essence of Mahabharata):** Srimad Bhagavatam is graced by the author of Mahabharata himself, Sage Veda Vyasa, and is the essence of the Bhagavad Gita.

3) **Brahma Sutras:** Srimad Bhagavatam is the commentary of the Brahma Sutras, written by Sage Veda Vyasa himself - e.g. the first shloka expounds on the second Brahma Sutra.

## Other Specialities of Srimad Bhagavatam
- It is the harmonized form (sāma roopa) of the Puranas
- “Purāṇam Brahma Sammittam” (SB 1.3.40): Srimad Bhagavatam is equivalent to the Vedas
- “Nigama kalpa tarōr galitam phalam” (SB 1.1.3): It is the fruit of all Vedas in a form accessible to all

---

## Srimad Bhagavatam is Bhagavan Himself
- **Padma Purana describes the greatness of Srimad Bhagavatam in six chapters (Srimad Bhagavata Mahatyam)**
  - 3rd chapter details a conversation between Sri Krishna and Uddhava
  - As Krishna is about to return to Vaikunta, Uddhava asks Him not to go in order to protect his devotees from Kali Yuga
  - Krishna says that he shall remain on Earth in the form of Srimad Bhagavatam
