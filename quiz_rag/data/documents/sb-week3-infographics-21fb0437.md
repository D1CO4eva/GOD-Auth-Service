### Four Episodes of Its Greatness

**Told by Veda Vyasa Bhagavan to reveal the greatness of Srimad Bhagavatam's glory.**

1. **Parikshith & the Nectar:**
   Devas offered their amrutha so Parikshith would drink it, hoping to receive Srimad Bhagavatam's nectar in exchange. Shuka Maharishi refused, saying that Bhagavatam nectar is far superior to Deva Nectar
   **Purport:** Bhagavata amrutha bestows moksha/Vaikunta itself.

2. **Brahmaji & the Measuring:**
   Brahmaji weighed every divine scripture against Srimad Bhagavatam on a balance. The others barely tipped the scales - Srimad Bhagavatam is Bhagavan Himself.
   **Purport:** A means is greater if it's the easiest path, or a sure one. The Bhagavatam is both- it isn't just a path to Bhagavan. It is Bhagavan.

3. **Narada & Bhakti Devi:**
   The jnana yoga of reading Srimad Bhagavatam aloud over 7 days- as the tree's essence is only enjoyed as the fruit.
   **Purport:** Essence of scripture; essence of Vedanta. Krishna bhakti alone leads to gnana and vairagya.

4. **Atmadeva:**
   Dhundhukari, who returned as a ghost, is liberated when Gokarna recites Srimad Bhagavatam over seven days to him.
   **Purport:** Regardless of one's past, they can be fully purified and liberated simply by hearing Srimad Bhagavatam.
