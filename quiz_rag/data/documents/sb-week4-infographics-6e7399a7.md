### The Story of Atmadeva

1. Atmadeva and his wife Dhulidul had everything, except a child. A great sanyasi gave them a divine fruit, but fearing pregnancy Dhuludi secretly fed it to their cow instead

2. The cow bore a divine child, Gokarna with cow-like ears. Dhuludi's sister’s baby, Dhundhukari was passed off as her own and he grew to be wicked and cruel while Gokarna grew wise and devoted.

3. Grieved by Dhundhukari's ways, Atmadeva renounced the world and attained the Lord’s feet by reading the 10th canto of Srimad Bhagavatam, on the advice of Gokarna. Dhundhukari wasted his life in sin and, when his money ran out, was beaten to death, becoming a ghost tormented by hunger and thirst.

4. Gokarna arranged a saptaha of Srimad Bhagavatam, for Dhundhukari’s sake. The ghost entered a hollow bamboo with seven nodes to listen to the divine kathā, one node bursting open each evening.

5. By the seventh day all the nodes had opened. Dhundhukari was freed from his sins, attained a divine form, and went to the Lord’s abode, followed by Gokarna.

**Even sacred rites like śraddha at holy places could not free Dhundhukari, but sincerely hearing Srimad Bhagavatam for seven days did. From the most spiritual (Gokarna) to the most sinful (Dhundhukari), every soul is uplifted by this great Purāṇa.**
