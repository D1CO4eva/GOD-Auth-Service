## Srimad Bhagavatam

- **Unique:** has 10 LakshanAs rather than 5
- **Ashraya** is the main subject
- **Each Canto** covers a dominant Lakshana
- With 12 Cantos and 10 LakshanAs, **the first two Cantos are the Preface and Introduction**.
- Each canto has many chapters which deal with a distinct subject or a story or a distinct portion of the story in line with the canto’s characteristic subject (lakshanā).

## The 6 Questions
Suta Pauranika was invited to speak on our Puranas during the interval periods of the yagna. Shaunaka Rishis ask 6 questions to Suta Pauranika to elicit the essence of all scriptures.

1. What is the ultimate good for the people in this Kali Yuga?
2. Why did Bhagavan descend as the son of Devaki and Vasudeva?
3. Please describe the Gambheera leelas of Bhagavan [His leela of Creation, sustenance and dissolution of the universe].
4. Describe all his incarnations
5. Describe the leelas Bhagavan did along with Balarama
6. Where did Dharma take refuge after Bhagavan’s leela samvarana (departure)?

- **1. Hari Bakthi (Devotion to Lord Hari)**
- **2. Bhagavan descended to nourish devotion.**
- **3. Gambheera Leela is discussed in Canto 2 and 3**
- **4. Various Avatars are described in all the Cantos/Chapters (Varaha, Matsya and Kurma/8, Narasimha/7 etc.)**
- **5. The sports of Bhagavan with Balarama are described in Canto 10.**
- **6. In this Age of Kali, Dharma has sought refuge in the name of the Lord!**
