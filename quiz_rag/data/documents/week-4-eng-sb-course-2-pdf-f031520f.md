4.1 Story of Atmadeva
Purport of the story:
This  story  teaches  something  very  important:  Even  a  person  as  sinful  as
Dhundhukari — a thief, a cruel man who spent his life in bad company — could be
completely  puriﬁed  and  bestowed  liberation,  simply  through  sincerely  listening  to
Srimad Bhagavatam for seven days. Even sacred rites like śrāddha performed at holy
places,  were  not  enough  to  free  him.  But  the  pure  hearing  of  this  Srimad
Bhagavatam accomplished what nothing else could.
This shows us the true greatness of Srimad Bhagavatam — it has the power to burn
away even the worst of sins, and to lead any sincere listener, regardless of their past,

4.2  Lineage of Srimad Bhagavatam
The four stories detailing Srimad Bhagavata Mahatmayam in Padma Purana were a
result of the discussion at Naimishāranyam (known also as Nimsar today) between
Shaunaka rishi and Ugrashravas Suta.
In a nutshell, Srimad Bhagavatam:
a) Dispels fear of samsāra - the cycle of birth and death (naturally)
b) Enhances devotion in heart (effortlessly)
c)
d) Puriﬁes mind thoroughly (painlessly)
e)
all because:
• Srimad Bhagavatam is the essence, sum and substance of all scriptures.
• Was  revealed  by  Sage  Veda  Vyasa  as  the  last  Purana  -  his  ﬁnal  work,  and  so

Is the result of heaps of past punya (ﬂips it up!)

Increases Krishna’s love towards us (steadily)

covered all his earlier words.

• Srimad Bhagavatam is Krishna Himself.
Vittala  came  from  Dwarakādeesha  and  is  Krishna  Himself  as  archāvatāra,  right?
Likewise, Srimad Bhagavatam also came from the lotus lips of Vāsudeva and is Sri
Vāsudeva Himself!

We shall now see the divine lineage of  Srimad Bhagavatam’s transmission!
1. Sri Vāsudeva
2. Sri Brahma ji
3. Sri Narada Maharishi
4. Sri Veda Vyasa Maharishi
5. Sri Shuka Muni
6. Parikshit Maharaja & Ugrashravas Suta
7. Shaunaka Rishi
Veda Vyasa Bhagavan added all the events of 1 to 7 and three more as introduction
shlokas, and we now have Srimad Bhagavatam in the form we see today.

The First Lineage of Transmission

The blue areas happened in divine higher worlds, beyond the earth we live in; the
orange  areas  are  in  our  world  as  we  know  it.  The  transmission  from  Narada
Maharishi to Sage Veda Vyasa is the divine bridge between this world and the ones
above.
The lineage of almost all Sanatana Dharma traditions have this ‘bridge’ between the
higher worlds to our earth - through medium of ‘yoga dasa’ - which is the reason for
its effective divinity and eternity.

4.3  Structure of Srimad Bhagavatam
Total number of Cantos (skandhās): 12
Total number of Chapters (adhyāyas): 335
Total number of Verses (shlokas): 18,000
So, numbering code 1.2.23 means 1st Canto, 2nd Chapter, 23rd Verse.

These are the prime speakers and listeners of Srimad Bhagavatam, and the verses
covered by each of them.
